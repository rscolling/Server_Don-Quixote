About American Chestnut Labs

Built for businesses with real roots

The American chestnut once dominated the Eastern forests — resilient, productive, deeply established. Then it was lost. We named our practice after its comeback: the work of rebuilding something strong using modern science without losing what made it great in the first place.

That's what we do for mid-market and small businesses. Your existing systems, workflows, and institutional knowledge are assets. We help you connect them to agentic AI — intelligently, without ripping everything out.
